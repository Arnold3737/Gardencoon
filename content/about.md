# About Garden State Coon

Garden State Coon is an established Maine Coon cattery based in Edison, New Jersey. We are a small, dedicated breeding program devoted to raising healthy, well-socialized Maine Coon kittens with the gentle temperament and striking looks the breed is loved for.

## Our Philosophy

We believe a great kitten begins long before it is born. Our breeding cats come from registered lines and are health-screened, and every kitten is raised underfoot in our home — not in cages. From their first days, our kittens are surrounded by the everyday sounds of family life, so they grow into confident, affectionate companions.

## How We Raise Our Kittens

Each litter receives round-the-clock attention. Kittens are litter-box trained, accustomed to gentle handling, and introduced to other cats and household routines before they ever go home. We follow health-focused breeding practices and work closely with our veterinarian throughout each kitten's early life.

## Where We Are

We are located in Edison, NJ 08817, just a short drive from Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR). Our central New Jersey location makes it convenient for families across the region — and beyond — to welcome a Garden State Coon kitten into their home.
