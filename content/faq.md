# Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I reserve a kitten?
Reach out to us at hello@gardenstatecoon.com to express interest in a specific kitten or an upcoming litter. We will walk you through our reservation process and answer any questions you have.

## How much do your kittens cost?
Pricing is available upon request. Please contact us and we will be glad to share current details for the kitten you are interested in.

## Are your cats registered?
Our breeding cats come from registered lines. We are happy to discuss the background of our cats when you get in touch.

## Are your kittens healthy?
Yes — we follow health-focused breeding practices, our breeding cats are health-screened, and every kitten is examined by our veterinarian and receives age-appropriate vaccinations before going home.

## Do you ship kittens?
We can arrange travel for kittens within the United States. Our location in Edison, NJ is convenient to Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR). See our Shipping page for details.

## When can a kitten come home?
Kittens stay with us until they are fully weaned, well-socialized, and have completed their early veterinary care. We will let you know the expected go-home timing for your kitten.

## What is included when I bring my kitten home?
Your kitten goes home litter-trained and well-socialized, with a record of veterinary care and vaccinations to share with your own veterinarian.

## Can I visit?
We would love to tell you more about our kittens and how they are raised. Contact us at hello@gardenstatecoon.com to discuss arrangements.
