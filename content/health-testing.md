# Health & Testing

The health of our cats and kittens is at the heart of everything we do at Garden State Coon. We follow health-focused breeding practices and work closely with our veterinarian throughout each kitten's early life.

## Health-Screened Breeding Cats

Our breeding cats come from registered lines and are health-screened. We select our cats with care, considering temperament, conformation, and overall wellness, so that our kittens have the best possible start.

## What Every Kitten Receives

Before going home, each kitten:
- Is examined by our veterinarian
- Receives age-appropriate vaccinations
- Is dewormed on schedule
- Is litter-box trained and well-socialized

## A Smooth Transition Home

We provide a record of each kitten's veterinary care and vaccinations so your own veterinarian has a clear history from day one. We recommend scheduling a wellness check shortly after your kitten comes home.

## Our Commitment

We are committed to health-focused breeding practices and to raising kittens in a clean, loving home environment. If you have questions about the health and care of our cats, we are always glad to talk — contact us at hello@gardenstatecoon.com.
