# Our Breeding Cats

Our breeding program is built on a small group of carefully chosen Maine Coons from registered lines. All of our breeding cats are health-screened and are cherished members of our family first and foremost.

## Hudson — Sire (King)
- **Color:** Brown classic tabby
- Hudson is our resident king: a large, gentle tom with the classic Maine Coon profile, lush coat, and an unmistakably sweet temperament. He passes on his calm, friendly nature and substantial bone structure.

## Willow — Dam (Queen)
- **Color:** Blue smoke
- Willow is an elegant queen with a soft, smoky coat and an attentive, loving disposition. She is a devoted mother who raises confident, well-adjusted kittens.

## Juniper — Dam (Queen)
- **Color:** Silver shaded
- Juniper is a striking silver queen with a shimmering coat and a graceful, gentle personality. Her kittens are known for their beauty and their relaxed, people-oriented temperament.

All of our breeding cats come from registered lines and are raised with health-focused breeding practices. We are happy to discuss our cats and their backgrounds — reach out at hello@gardenstatecoon.com.
