# Shipping & Travel

Garden State Coon is located in Edison, New Jersey. We are happy to help your new kitten travel safely to its home across the United States.

## Local Pickup

Families in New Jersey and the surrounding region are welcome to arrange pickup directly with us. Our central NJ location is easy to reach.

## Travel Within the United States

For families farther away, we can arrange travel for your kitten. Our location is convenient to Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR), one of the region's major airports, which makes coordinating travel straightforward.

## Safe & Considerate Travel

The comfort and safety of our kittens always come first. We will work with you to choose travel arrangements that are calm and low-stress, and a kitten only travels once it is healthy and ready.

## Questions?

Travel details depend on your location and timing. Contact us at hello@gardenstatecoon.com and we will help plan the best way to bring your kitten home.
