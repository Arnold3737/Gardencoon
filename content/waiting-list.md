# Join Our Waiting List

Our kittens are raised in small, carefully planned litters, and demand often exceeds availability. Joining our waiting list is the best way to be among the first to hear about upcoming kittens.

## How It Works

When you join the waiting list, you let us know what you are hoping for — a particular sex, color, or simply a healthy, loving companion. As new litters are planned and born, we reach out to families on the list first.

## No Pressure

Being on the waiting list places you under no obligation. It simply keeps you informed and gives you an early opportunity when kittens become available.

## Get Started

To join, email us at hello@gardenstatecoon.com with a little about yourself and what you are looking for. We will be in touch as kittens of similar lines become available.
