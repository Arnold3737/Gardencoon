Our Health Testing Program

Health is the foundation of our breeding program. Our breeding cats come from registered lines and are health-screened before any planned litter. Every kitten is examined by our veterinarian before going home, and we provide a record of veterinary care for your own vet.

Deworming & Care

Kittens are dewormed on schedule, litter-box trained, and well-socialized in our home.

Veterinary Care Record

We provide a record of veterinary care for your own vet, so your kitten's care continues seamlessly at home.

⚗

Ringworm Culture & Fecal Panel

A ringworm culture and comprehensive fecal panel are performed to ensure each kitten is free from fungal infections and intestinal parasites.

⚛

Deworming & Vaccination

Every kitten receives deworming ×3 (Pyrantel + Fenbendazole) and FVRCP vaccine ×2 before going home. This protocol ensures strong immune protection from day one.

Our Breeding Cats

Our home cattery is built around two cats: Hudson, our sire, a Brown classic tabby, and Willow, our dam, a Blue smoke. We are glad to discuss our breeding cats' health background with prospective families when you get in touch.
