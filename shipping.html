Shipping & Delivery

Safe, comfortable delivery options for your new Maine Coon kitten anywhere in the United States

Delivery Options

We offer four ways to get your kitten home safely. Every option includes a health certificate, a travel carrier, and a comfort kit for the journey. Please note: a heat embargo is in effect June 15 – August 31 for air cargo shipments to protect your kitten during extreme temperatures.

How It Works

1

Local Pickup — Free

Pick up your kitten in person from our home in Edison, New Jersey. We'll walk you through care instructions, send you home with a kitten kit, and make sure you're fully prepared. No additional cost.

2

Ground Transport

Door-to-door ground delivery within driving distance of Edison, New Jersey. Your kitten travels in a climate-controlled vehicle with an experienced pet transporter. Includes a travel carrier and comfort kit.

3

Air Cargo

Airline cargo service to major US airports, departing from Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR). Your kitten flies in a pressurized, temperature-controlled cabin. Price varies by destination. Heat embargo applies June 15 – August 31.

4

Flight Nanny

A trusted flight nanny accompanies your kitten in-cabin on a commercial flight to your nearest airport. The most personal and comfortable option. Your kitten is hand-delivered at the gate.
